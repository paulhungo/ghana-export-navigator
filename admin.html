Navigator Admin — Add a Product

Research a new product, fill the fields, generate the JSON entry

How this works: when you research a new product (e.g. from official agency sites), fill this form and generate its record. Then paste the generated object into data\products.json (inside the "products" array), run _build\pack_data.py, and push. The live site updates for every user in about a minute.
